Source: NIMBLE official repository URL: https://github.com/reyuwei/NIMBLE_model Captured: 2026-09-26 We present NIMBLE, a non-rigid parametric hand model that includes bones and muscles, bringing 3D hand model to a new level of realism. By enforcing the inner bones and muscles to match anatomic and kinematic rules, NIMBLE can animate 3D hands to new poses at unprecedented realism. Use handle_collision=True to make sure skin is always outside muscle.
